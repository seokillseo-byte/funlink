# FunLink — Approved Scope

## In scope
- Authentication and accounts.
- Workspaces, membership, roles and tenant isolation.
- Short-link creation and management.
- Redirect resolution and click-event capture.
- Custom domains and domain verification.
- Link analytics and reporting.
- Campaign and UTM management.
- QR code generation/management.
- Customer workspace and dashboard shown in approved design.
- Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans.
- Monthly/yearly billing presentation.
- VNPay payment processing.
- Usage quota and subscription lifecycle.
- Bilingual public website and technical SEO.
- CMS shown in DESIGN-02.
- Approved admin operations.

## Out of scope unless separately approved
- Additional payment providers.
- AI content generation or AI marketing automation.
- Social network publishing/automation.
- Affiliate network integrations.
- New analytics products not in the approved module specification.
- Microservices or multiple independently deployed products.
- Mobile applications/browser extensions.
- Any feature not in the Blueprint or active approved Issue.

## Pricing and commercial data
Plan names and four-card layout are locked. Exact live prices, quotas, limits, Enterprise terms and billing policy must be explicitly confirmed before being treated as production truth. Screenshot figures are not automatically authoritative commercial configuration.

## Payment
VNPay is the sole approved payment provider. Do not add PayPal, Binance Pay, Stripe or other providers.
